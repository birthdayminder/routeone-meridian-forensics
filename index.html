The Project Cost Black Hole

First anomaly: Cost of Goods Sold was 78% of revenue. Industry standard: 55-60%. We dug into the General Ledger.

General Ledger Sample: Q4 2025 Production Costs
Date Account Description Amount
10/15/25 Production Labor Studio A - Project X $12,400

11/03/25 Professional Services Subcontractor edit ← WRONG ACCOUNT $14,200
11/12/25 Production Labor Studio B - Project Z $9,100

THE DISCOVERY

$47,000 in production costs were miscoded as operating expenses. This artificially inflated their overhead and made projects look unprofitable.

Project X was billed at $45,000. Actual costs: $38,400 (including the miscoded $6,400). True margin: 14.7% (not -8.3% as shown in books).

What They Thought

Projects were losing money. Need to raise prices or shut down production.

The Reality

Projects were profitable, just misclassified. +$47K found.
